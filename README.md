# ColtCreate

A lightweight, single-file creative and productivity office suite **built for Muir Middle School (HVS)** by **Henry Green**. Part of the **ColtApps** ecosystem, **ColtCreate** brings document editing, presentations, spreadsheets, video editing, digital painting, and classroom forms together in one fast, Chromebook-friendly web app—no account or subscription required.

---

## Quick Access

### For HVS Students (No Download Required)
- **ColtCreate Web Suite:** [sites.google.com/hvs.org/coltcreate](https://sites.google.com/hvs.org/coltcreate)

*(Must be signed into your HVS Google account to access the Google Sites portal.)*

### For Non-HVS Students — Download & Run Locally (Downloads Folder)
1. Click the **Code** button on this repository and select **Download ZIP** (or download `coltcreate.html` directly).
2. Open your computer's **Downloads** folder and extract the ZIP file if needed.
3. Double-click `coltcreate.html` to launch ColtCreate directly in any modern web browser—works completely offline with zero installation.

## Included Applications

- **ColtHub (Home & Local Drive):** Central launcher with Muir Middle School starter templates, active project drive, auto-save status, and 1-click `.json` workspace backup/restore.
- **ColtWriter *(Word Equivalent)*:** Rich-text word processor with formatting controls (headings, alignment, lists, tables, colors), live word/character/reading-time counters, page zoom, and `.html`, `.txt`, and Print/PDF export.
- **ColtSlides *(PowerPoint Equivalent)*:** Presentation deck builder featuring a slide sorter, 4 switchable slide layouts, 4 themes (including **Muir Colts Navy & Gold**), inline editing, and fullscreen **Present Mode** with arrow-key navigation.
- **ColtSheets *(Excel Equivalent)*:** Interactive spreadsheet grid with a live formula engine (`=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, `=MIN()`, `=MAX()`, `=COUNT()`, and cell arithmetic), AutoSum, live bar chart visualizer, and `.csv` export.
- **ColtStudio *(Clipchamp Equivalent)*:** Multi-track timeline motion & video editor with an HTML5 `<canvas>` stage, kinetic text overlays, scene backgrounds, Web Audio API background synth track, timeline scrubber, and `.png` frame export.
- **ColtPaint *(MS Paint / Designer Equivalent)*:** Digital drawing studio with Brush, Eraser, Line, Rectangle, Circle, Stamp tools, Muir Navy & Gold color swatches, undo history, and `.png` image export.
- **ColtForms & ColtNotes *(Forms + Sticky Notes)*:** Live classroom poll and quiz builder with instant response bar charts paired with a sticky-note study board.
